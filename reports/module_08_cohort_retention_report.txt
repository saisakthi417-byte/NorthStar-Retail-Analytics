BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
-----------------------------------
1. Compare retention between join-month cohorts before choosing customer engagement campaigns.
2. Investigate cohorts with weaker repeat purchase rates and test relevant re-engagement offers.
3. Evaluate cohort results alongside acquisition source, channel, discounting, and return behaviour in later modules.
4. Interpret the newest cohorts cautiously because they have had less time to make repeat purchases.

Method note: Cohorts are based on customer JoinDate, as required by the project brief. Retention means a customer has at least one recorded purchase in the relevant month. Repeat purchaser means at least two distinct transaction IDs after the recorded join date.
ObservedSales is the sum of LineAmount for valid post-join activity; confirm the business definition of LineAmount before interpreting it as net sales or profit.
